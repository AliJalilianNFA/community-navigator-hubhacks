# Community Navigator

## RBC HubHacks Practice Prototype

### Problem

Residents often need to search across multiple websites and organizations
to find community services, public services, and essential places.

At the same time, service providers may have limited visibility into
which services are most requested, which communities have the highest
demand, and where service gaps exist.

---

## Our Solution

Community Navigator is a prototype that provides two connected experiences:

### 1. Resident View

Residents can search for:

- Community support
- Public services
- Hospitals and health services
- Libraries
- Schools
- Shopping centres
- Employment support
- Housing support
- Transportation
- Food support

Users can filter results based on their area, needs, urgency,
accessibility requirements, and cost preferences.

The system then recommends relevant services.

### 2. Organization View

Organizations can use an analytics dashboard to understand:

- Total service demand
- Served vs unmet requests
- Demand by area
- Demand by service category
- Trends over time
- Average wait times
- Satisfaction levels
- Communities with the highest service gaps

The system can also generate simple data-driven recommendations
to help organizations prioritize resources.

---

## Target Users

### Residents

People looking for community, government, health, education,
and essential services.

### Organizations

Community organizations, public-sector organizations,
service providers, and decision makers.

---

## Core Value

For residents:

Find the right service faster.

For organizations:

Understand demand and make better data-driven decisions.

---

## Prototype Scope

This prototype uses synthetic data for demonstration purposes.

It is designed to work:

- Offline
- On laptops
- On mobile screens
- Without user login
- Without a live database
- Without external APIs

The architecture is intentionally simple so it can be adapted
quickly to a real hackathon problem statement and dataset.
